Free reflection vs paid

The free reflection first helps you get clear on what is going on. Want to go further, together or alone? That is paid: Reflection+ for yourself, or a conversation with the other person.

Questionnaire and follow-up

Anonymity

With every submission you can choose whether your name is visible to the other participants.
