For business & professionals

Conflict mediation on an objective basis, not guesswork

For companies, HR teams, therapists, teachers and lawyers who want to start a conversation between two (or more) other people, without being a party themselves.

Why this makes your work easier

Today you often start a mediation with two sides of the same story that you first have to hear out separately, often in separate meetings. Middlepoint does that for you: both parties share their side separately and in complete calm, and you get an objective overview ready before your first session begins. That saves time, and you start the conversation with facts instead of guesswork.
