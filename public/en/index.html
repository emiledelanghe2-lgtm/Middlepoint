Why Middlepoint exists

We try to understand each other

We try to understand each other, but that is not always easy. Especially when it comes to truly grasping what is going on for the other person and what they feel, something often slips away. Not because we do not care, but because we rarely take the time to stop and think about it together.

Research from the University of Chicago showed that couples and close friends did not understand each other better in conversations than complete strangers did, even though they were convinced that they were understood.

Middlepoint gives that time back. Start with a free, personal reflection and first find out for yourself what is going on. Does a conversation turn out to be worthwhile? Then each of you tells your side of the story separately, in peace and quiet, and we place both sides next to each other in a clear, honest document.

In doing so we always choose honesty over false balance. We treat both sides with equal respect, but we do not pretend that everyone is always equally right. Not to be proven right, but to understand each other again.
